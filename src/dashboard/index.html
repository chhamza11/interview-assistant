Connect the AI that writes your answers Sign in once with the subscription you already have. Your browser opens, and this page carries on by itself.

Everything here does two jobs: it tells the AI what the meeting is about (and your CV / company profile), and the names and jargon get fed into the speech recogniser so it stops mishearing them.

Persistent CV & Profile: When you click Save brief & default CV profile, your CV, company, role, and jargon are saved as your default profile and automatically pre-filled in every future meeting and internal interview.
Anything marked DON'T SAY or NOTE stays private — it reads those to decide what to say, and never says them out loud.
